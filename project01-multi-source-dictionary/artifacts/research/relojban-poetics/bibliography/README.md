## Verification vocabulary

`metadata` means the identity/record was inspected. `abstract` means only the abstract or publisher summary was assessed. `passage` means named sections were inspected. `full-study` requires checking methods, results, limitations, and relevant supplements. `replicated` additionally requires an independent reconstruction. No entry currently has `full-study` or `replicated` status. Access and verification are separate: an accessible paper is not automatically a verified finding.

## Citation practice

Each reading note records source ID, URL/DOI, edition/version, acquisition date, passage locator, a short quotation or clearly marked paraphrase, what it supports, and what remains unverified. Keep third-party works under their own terms; retain permitted locators rather than assuming a repository license covers them. Do not label an inaccessible or search-only source as fully read.
